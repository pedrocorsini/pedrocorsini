### 🎬 CorsaBox
**Your next watch starts here.**

A personal diary and community for movies, series and anime. Discover something new, keep track of every episode and share your take.

- **Discover:** browse the TMDB catalog, check streaming availability in Brazil and build your watchlist.
- **Remember:** track episodes, record viewing dates and keep ratings and reviews together.
- **Connect:** publish reviews, find friends and make your profile your own.

React frontend, Spring Boot REST API, Google sign-in and JWT authentication.
